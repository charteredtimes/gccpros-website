How many GCCs are there in India?

By the GCCPROs research team · Updated 23 September 2026

India has 2,503 Global Capability Centres as of 22 September 2026, on the GCCPROs verified count. The most widely quoted industry figure, from the nasscom and Zinnov India GCC Landscape report, is 2,117 GCCs operating 3,728 units in FY26. Both describe the same industry. They were counted at different times, and they count slightly different things.

Which Indian cities have the most GCCs?

Bengaluru has the most, with 1,377 (55% of India’s total). Six cities hold most of India’s GCCs, and 57 Indian cities have at least one.

Why do sources give different numbers?

Three ordinary reasons, and none of them means anyone is wrong.

The India GCC counts most often quoted, and what each one measures.
SourceIndia figureWhat it counts
GCCPROs verified database2,503Each GCC once for India, updated 22 September 2026
nasscom and Zinnov2,117 GCCs, 3,728 unitsIndia GCC Landscape report, FY26
JLLOver 2,500 expectedA projection in its India GCC guide, 2026
  1. The date. The industry is adding centres every month. A report finalised for a financial year will be lower than a count taken in September 2026.
  2. The definition. Sources draw the line differently on what qualifies as a GCC, for example on the smallest centres and on how units inside a larger group are treated.
  3. Companies or units. nasscom and Zinnov report 2,117 GCCs but 3,728 units, because many GCCs run several sites. GCCPROs counts each GCC once for India; the 870 GCCs with offices in more than one city appear once in each of those city figures.
How we count

How the GCCPROs number is built

Every centre in the GCCPROs database is verified individually, with up to 46 fields held per centre: location, parent company, industry, the work the centre runs, headcount band, maturity, year established and leadership.

A GCC with offices in more than one city is counted in each of those cities, so the city figures add up to more than the national total. Every city, region and country total counts each GCC only once. The figures on this page are rebuilt from the live database every night, so they always match the database, the GCC India Atlas and every other page on this site.

99% are owned outright

Of the 2,399 Indian centres with a recorded setup model, 2,371 are captives the parent company owns. 20 are recorded as Build-Operate-Transfer.

61% employ 500 or fewer

Of the centres with a known headcount band. 108 employ more than 5,000 people.

47% own real work

Of the centres with a recorded maturity level, 47% are Gen 3 or Gen 4: they own a product, an engineering charter or a global process end to end.

Questions

GCC numbers, answered

How many GCCs are there in India?

2,503 as of 22 September 2026, on the GCCPROs verified count. The most widely quoted industry figure, from the nasscom and Zinnov India GCC Landscape report, is 2,117 GCCs operating 3,728 units in FY26. Both describe the same industry; they were counted at different times and count slightly different things.

How many GCCs are there in the world?

GCCPROs verifies 4,611 Global Capability Centres across 44 countries. India holds 2,503 of them, more than any other country.

Which Indian city has the most GCCs?

Bengaluru, with 1,377 (55% of India’s total). It is followed by Bengaluru 1,377, Pune 559, Hyderabad 522, Delhi NCR 478, Mumbai 364, Chennai 364. A GCC with offices in more than one city is counted in each of those cities, so the city figures add up to more than the national total.

Is the number of GCCs in India still growing?

Yes. 458 of the centres GCCPROs verifies in India were established from 2023 onward. Hyderabad is growing fastest relative to its size: 22% of its centres were set up from 2023 onward.

Why do different sources give different numbers of GCCs in India?

Three ordinary reasons. They were counted at different dates. They draw the line differently on what qualifies as a GCC. And some sources count companies while others count their individual units: nasscom and Zinnov report 2,117 GCCs but 3,728 units. GCCPROs counts each GCC once for India, and once in every city where it has an office.

How many people work in India’s GCCs?

nasscom and Zinnov put GCC employment in India at about 2.36 million in FY26. GCCPROs does not publish a national headcount total, because not every centre discloses its headcount; it records headcount bands instead, and 61% of the centres with a known size employ 500 people or fewer.
