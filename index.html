GCC Industry Council

A professional institution, not a commercial platform.

An invite-only, non-commercial council of GCC leaders. Every member is in full-time employment at a GCC: no vendors, no consultants, no commercial interests.

The GCC Industry Council is a peer community of professionals in full-time employment across the world’s Global Capability Centres. Membership carries no commercial obligation, and the Council operates as an independent knowledge-sharing and advocacy body.From the Community Charter

Peer-to-peer only

No consulting, vendor relationships or commercial introductions through the Council.

Employer-neutral

Members take part as individual professionals, not as representatives of their employers.

Members-only discretion

What is shared in the room stays among members, in good faith.
