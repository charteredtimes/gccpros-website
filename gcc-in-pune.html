The number

How many GCCs are in Pune?

Pune is India’s second-largest GCC city, with 535 centres, 22.3% of the national total, narrowly ahead of Hyderabad.

It is the automotive capital. 57 of India’s 122 automotive GCCs are in Pune (46.7%), alongside 60 industrial and manufacturing centres.

Engineering is the job. 404 of Pune’s 535 centres run engineering or R&D work, 75.5%, the highest share among India’s five largest GCC cities, and 74 run manufacturing technology.

How we count. Counted at unit level. A GCC that operates units in more than one city is counted once in every city where it has a unit, so city figures sum to more than the national total. Each scope's own total is a distinct count of GCCs, never a sum of its cities. A centre operating in Pune and one other city appears in both, which is why India’s city figures add up to more than 2,401. 847 of the 2,401 GCCs in India run units in more than one city.

Questions

GCCs in Pune, answered.

How many GCCs are there in Pune?

GCCPROs has 535 verified Global Capability Centres with a unit in Pune, which makes it India’s second-largest GCC city after Bengaluru. Many of these also operate in Mumbai or Bengaluru; each unit is counted in the city where it sits.

What makes Pune different from Bengaluru?

Composition. Pune skews industrial: automotive (57 centres) and industrial engineering and manufacturing (60) together account for 21.9% of its base, against 10.5% in Bengaluru.

How mature are Pune’s GCCs?

187 of Pune’s centres are Gen 3, meaning they own a product, an engineering charter or a global process end to end. That is 35% of the city’s base, the highest Gen 3 concentration of India’s five largest GCC cities.
