The number

How many GCCs are in Mumbai?

Mumbai has 358 GCCs, 14.9% of India’s total. Navi Mumbai adds a further 39 and Thane 11.

It is India’s financial GCC capital. Banking, fintech and capital markets (97) plus insurance (21) make up 33% of the city’s centres, against 16.3% nationally.

That shows up in the work. 25.7% of Mumbai’s GCCs run risk and compliance and 41.9% run finance and accounting — both the highest shares in the country, against 10.4% and 22.6% nationally.

How we count. Counted at unit level. A GCC that operates units in more than one city is counted once in every city where it has a unit, so city figures sum to more than the national total. Each scope's own total is a distinct count of GCCs, never a sum of its cities. A centre operating in Mumbai and one other city appears in both — which is why India’s city figures add up to more than 2,401. 847 of the 2,401 GCCs in India run units in more than one city.

Questions

GCCs in Mumbai, answered.

How many GCCs are there in Mumbai?

GCCPROs has 358 verified Global Capability Centres with a unit in Mumbai proper. Navi Mumbai (39) and Thane (11) are counted separately because they are distinct operating locations.

Is Mumbai only a BFSI location for GCCs?

It is heavily BFSI — 33% of its centres — but professional services (37) and SaaS and software (35) are both substantial.

Why does Mumbai have fewer GCCs than Pune?

The database records where units are, not why. What it shows is that Pune has 535 centres to Mumbai’s 358, and that the gap is almost entirely engineering: Pune has 404 centres running engineering or R&D against Mumbai’s 191.
