The number

How many GCCs are in Delhi NCR?

Across Gurugram, Noida, New Delhi, Greater Noida, Manesar and Faridabad, 478 GCCs operate in the NCR, 19.1% of India’s total. The individual city figures add up to 534, because many GCCs have offices in more than one NCR city; the regional total counts each of them once.

Gurugram carries most of it, with 320 centres, ahead of Noida (130) and New Delhi (63).

Its edge is advisory and services. The NCR holds 46 of India’s 116 professional-services GCCs, 39.7%, and they make up 9.6% of its base, against 4.4% in Bengaluru.

How we count. A GCC with offices in more than one city is counted in each of those cities, so the city figures add up to more than the national total. Every city, region and country total counts each GCC only once. A centre operating in Delhi NCR and one other city appears in both, which is why India’s city figures add up to more than 2,503. 870 of the 2,503 GCCs in India have offices in more than one city.

Questions

GCCs in Delhi NCR, answered.

How many GCCs are there in Delhi NCR?

478 Global Capability Centres operate across the National Capital Region. Many have offices in two or more NCR cities, so adding up the figures for Gurugram, Noida, New Delhi, Greater Noida, Manesar and Faridabad would count them twice and give 534.

Which NCR city has the most GCCs?

Gurugram, with 320 centres, followed by Noida with 130 and New Delhi with 63.

What kind of work do NCR GCCs do?

Technology is the most common function (387 centres), but the NCR over-indexes on professional services and consulting (46 centres) and under-indexes sharply on cybersecurity, with only 5 centres against Bengaluru’s 71.
