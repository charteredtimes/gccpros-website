About this database

4,847 verified Global Capability Centres across 44 countries, 2,551 of them in India, with up to 46 fields on each: location, parent company, industry, the work the centre runs, headcount band, maturity, year established and named leadership. It is the largest verified GCC dataset we know of, and it is rebuilt from the live database every night.

The database is the source for every number published on this site. The GCC India Atlas, the six city pages, the research and the answer to how many GCCs there are in India are all generated from it, so they cannot drift apart.

Questions

The GCC database, answered

How many GCCs are in the GCCPROs database?

4,847 verified Global Capability Centres across 44 countries, of which 2,551 are in India. The count is rebuilt from the live database every night, so the figure on this page always matches the data behind it. How the India figure compares with other published counts.

What information is held on each GCC?

Up to 46 fields per centre: parent company and group, country, city and office addresses, industry, the domains the centre runs, headcount band, year established, maturity generation, legal structure and setup model, and named leadership where it is on the public record.

How is the data verified?

Every centre is checked individually rather than scraped in bulk. A company that runs centres in more than one city counts once in the national and global totals, and appears in the count for each of those cities. So the city figures add up to more than the national total, and no company is counted twice within one city. Each record carries a confidence level, and corrections are reviewed before they are published.

How often is it updated?

Continuously, and the whole site is rebuilt from it nightly. The figures on this page were last refreshed on 25 October 2026.

Can I export the data or use an API?

Yes. Search and filter in the browser, export what you need, and enterprise users can use the API. Access runs on credits, with packs for individuals and a plan for teams; the options are on this page.

Which countries are covered?

44 countries. India is the largest at 2,551 centres, followed by the Philippines, Poland, Mexico and Romania. The rest of the world accounts for the remainder of the 4,847 total.

Who uses it?

Companies planning or scaling a GCC, service providers selling to GCCs, investors and advisers sizing the market, state investment-promotion agencies, and GCC leaders benchmarking themselves against their peers.
