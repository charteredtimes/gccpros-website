Who was on the floor.

Protiviti joined as Silver Partner, represented by Managing Directors Nancy Bhatt and Tarun Arora. Questions

GCC events, answered.

What is the GCC Leadership Summit?

A closed-door meeting for the people who actually run Global Capability Centres, sitting with senior policy makers. No vendors are invited. The 2026 edition at the Conrad in Pune on 11 August brought more than 100 GCC leaders from more than 80 companies into one room, with the Chief Minister of Maharashtra as chief guest.

When and where is the next GCC event in India?

The next event is a breakfast roundtable in HITEC City, Hyderabad on Thursday, 1 October 2026, capped at twenty GCC site heads. The GCC Leadership Summit follows in Hyderabad on Friday, 20 November 2026 from 9:00 AM, invite only and capped at 200 seats.

What is the GCC breakfast roundtable in Hyderabad?

A curated, low-key breakfast table in HITEC City on Thursday, 1 October 2026, from 8:00 AM, hosted by the GCCPROs Founder and CEO. Only twenty GCC site heads are invited. There is no stage and no agenda of speeches, and it is by invitation.

Does GCCPROs run events outside India?

Yes. The Europe tour runs from Thursday, 29 October to Thursday, 5 November 2026, with roadshows and curated roundtables in London, the Netherlands, Germany and Switzerland. The first global summit follows in Dubai on 4 February 2027.

Is there a GCC event in Dubai?

Yes. The GCCPROs Global Summit is in Dubai on 4 February 2027, the first global edition. It brings companies planning a GCC together with senior GCC leaders and government officials.

Who attends a GCCPROs summit?

Serving GCC leaders and their leadership teams, with state policy makers and academic partners. At Pune, 19 CEOs joined the Chief Minister’s roundtable and four dignitaries spoke from the stage. We keep the numbers small on purpose, so people speak freely.

How do I get an invitation?

Register your interest on this page and say which summit and which city matter to you. Registering puts you in line for priority invitations to summits in your city. Invitations are issued by the GCCPROs team, and Hyderabad is capped at 200 seats.

Are vendors and service providers invited?

No. The summits are vendor-free by design. Pune was a closed-door, vendor-free forum and the format has not changed, because people only discuss what is not working when nobody present is selling something. Tier 1 academic institutions are invited to partner.
