GCCPROs Global Summit, Dubai.

Thursday, 4 February 2027 Dubai Invite only

Our first global summit takes the GCCPROs format outside India — a forum for GCC leadership and strategy with senior practitioners and heads of government.

The format

What makes this room different.

The first global edition

Three years of Indian summits, taken to the market where global capability strategy is increasingly set. India remains the centre of gravity; Dubai is where the conversation crosses borders.

Strategy, not showcase

Sessions on operating models, mandate expansion, talent strategy and the economics of multi-country capability footprints.

Practitioner-led throughout

Chaired by people who have built and run capability centres, with the same working-session format as the Indian editions.

The Dubai edition follows the GCC Leadership Summits in Pune (August 2026) and Hyderabad (November 2026). See the Pune recap.

The room

Who will be there.

  • Global heads of capability and shared services
  • GCC site and country heads from India, the Gulf, Europe and Asia
  • Heads of government and investment-promotion agencies
  • Enterprise leaders evaluating a multi-country footprint

Attendance is by invitation and confirmed individually. Registering your interest is the first step — we will come back to you with the details.

Partner with the Dubai summit.

Summit partner
