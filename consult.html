BEFORE YOU BOOK

Common questions

Will my name be public?

Public capability profiles are anonymised. Applications are private; applying does not publish your name, employer or contact details. GCCPROs reviews your full identity and credentials privately. Clients receive a limited capability profile that excludes your name, contact details, LinkedIn URL, employer names and ID documents. A distinctive career history or voice can still be recognisable; this is not a promise of complete anonymity.

Is $200–$500/hour the client price?

No. This is the honorarium range offered for selected expert engagements, before applicable deductions. Client quotes include expert time and the agreed matching, screening and engagement services. Taxes, preparation and cancellation terms must be stated in the quote.

Are conversations recorded or used to train AI?

Our default is no recording, transcription, publication or AI training. Any exception requires separate, explicit consent and a defined purpose, access policy and retention period.

Can I book one of these profiles now?

The profiles illustrate capability areas rather than available individual consultants. Select the experience you need and submit a project brief. Our team will confirm fit, availability, permitted topics and a client quote before any engagement is booked.

Can a consultation lead to a longer project?

Start with a specific question. If implementation support is needed, scope a consulting project or fractional mandate with agreed deliverables, fees and responsibilities. Fractional leadership typically requires a named appointment with the expert’s consent.

CONSULTING INTAKE · 16 SEPTEMBER 2026

Your details stay with the brief.

Chartered Times LLP, operating as GCCPROs, collects the details submitted in a client brief or private expert application. Expert records include legal name, email, phone, location, LinkedIn URL, employers, dates, skill evidence, detailed work profile, rate, availability, participation constraints and a government ID number and document. Aadhaar numbers are validated for accuracy, then stored masked (last four digits only).

Why we collect this

We use it to review your request or application, assess relevant experience and participation restrictions, arrange follow-up and manage the consulting relationship. Application receipt is not verification, acceptance into the network, a booking or approval of an hourly rate.

Who can receive it

Client briefs use our existing enquiry service, Supabase storage and operational emails delivered through Resend. Expert applications use a separate encrypted profile store and private document bucket in Supabase. Only allowlisted GCCPROs reviewers can retrieve the full profile or ID; access and review decisions are audited. Expert identity details and documents are not included in notification emails, public pages or client profiles. Our registration abuse check uses Cloudflare Turnstile. No identity document is sent to clients through this service.

Your choices

Keep confidential employer information and bank details out of these forms. Provide an identification document only in the dedicated expert registration step; never include it in a client brief. There is no marketing opt-in bundled into this request. Recording, transcription, research reuse and AI-training permission are not granted by submitting a form. We do not save unfinished form content in this page’s persistent browser storage.

Access, correction and withdrawal

You may request withdrawal and deletion, including removal of identity documents after verification. Documents remain private while retained for review; retention requests are handled by the GCCPROs team subject to applicable obligations. For access, correction, deletion requests or withdrawal from future opportunities, email admin@gccpros.com. Retention, legally required records and wider service privacy practices are described in the GCCPROs Privacy Policy. Engagement scope and commercial terms will be agreed separately.
