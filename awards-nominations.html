About the GCC Leadership Excellence Awards

The call for nominations recognises the people who build and run India’s Global Capability Centres, the site heads, function leads and transformation owners whose work rarely leaves the building. There is no fee to nominate, no category you can buy, and no pay-to-win tier. Every entry is read by practitioners from the GCC Industry Council.

The categories

GCC Leader of the Year

A site or country head who materially changed what their centre is trusted to own.

Emerging GCC of the Year

A centre set up in the last three years that scaled faster and further than its mandate implied.

Transformation Excellence

A move from support work to product, engineering or decision ownership, evidenced by outcomes.

Talent & Culture

Measurable progress on retention, capability building, or opening the centre to non-metro talent.

How entries are judged

  • Evidence over narrative: what changed, by how much, over what period.
  • Scope of ownership: the shift from executing a brief to owning the outcome.
  • Durability: whether the result survived a budget cycle and a leadership change.
  • Peer review: every shortlist is checked by working GCC leaders, not by sponsors.

Winners are announced at the GCC Leadership Summit and profiled in the GCCPROs newsroom. For questions about an entry, write to admin@gccpros.com.
