The day, in a line

One room. The people who actually run India’s GCCs. One day that moved the industry forward.

On 11 August 2026, GCCPROs convened its biggest gathering yet at Conrad, Pune, a closed-door, vendor-free forum where GCC Site Heads, Country Heads and Domain Heads sat with the country’s top policymakers. From the ceremonial lamp-lighting to frank panels on talent, innovation and what’s next, it was a day built on real conversation, not pitches. Here is how it unfolded.
