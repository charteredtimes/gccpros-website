The format

What makes this room different.

A room, not an audience

Roundtables and working sessions, not a stage and rows of chairs. Everyone in the room runs or builds a capability centre, so nobody has to explain the basics.

Unfiltered by design

We take pride in talking about what is not working, not only what is. That only happens when the room is invitation-only and the participants know each other are practitioners.

The people who decide

Site heads, country heads, function leaders and policy makers: the people with the mandate to move work, not the people sent to collect brochures.

In the room

  • GCC site heads and country heads
  • Function and domain leaders (technology, engineering, finance, operations)
  • State and central policy makers

Questions

The Hyderabad GCC summit, answered.

When and where is the Hyderabad GCC Leadership Summit?

Thursday, 26 November 2026, in Hyderabad, Telangana. The venue is confirmed directly with invited attendees. It is the second edition of the GCCPROs Leadership Summit, after Pune in August 2026.

Who attends the Hyderabad GCC summit?

Around 200 invited people: GCC site heads and country heads, function and domain leaders across technology, engineering, finance and operations, leaders setting up a new centre in India, state and central policy makers. It is a practitioner room, not a vendor floor.

How many GCCs are there in Hyderabad?

GCCPROs verifies 540 Global Capability Centres with an office in Hyderabad, 21.2% of India’s 2,552. 119 of them were established from 2023 onward, the highest recent-arrival share of India’s five largest GCC cities. See the full Hyderabad breakdown.

Is the Hyderabad GCC summit free to attend?

Yes. There is no ticket price. Seats are limited to 200 and attendance is by invitation, confirmed individually after you register your interest.

How is this different from other GCC events in Hyderabad?

The format is roundtables and working sessions rather than a stage and rows of chairs, the room is capped so it stays a peer conversation, and partnerships are limited per edition. Everyone present runs or builds a capability centre, so the discussion starts past the basics.

Can my company partner with or sponsor the Hyderabad summit?

Yes, in three forms: summit partner, session partner or category partner. Partnerships are deliberately limited per edition. See the partnership formats, or write to admin@gccpros.com and we will tell you honestly whether this is the right room for what you are trying to achieve.

Why Hyderabad

Hyderabad is India’s fastest-growing GCC city.

We are not guessing at that. GCCPROs verifies every Global Capability Centre in its database, office by office, and Hyderabad is where the recent arrivals are concentrated.

22% of Hyderabad’s centres were set up from 2023 onward, the highest recent-arrival share among India’s five largest GCC cities, against 15.4% for Bengaluru, 14.5% for Pune and 9.2% for Chennai. The city also holds two clear specialisms: 44 of India’s 98 pharma and life-sciences GCCs, and 51.5% of its centres run data, analytics or AI work, a higher share than any other major Indian city.

That mix is why the Hyderabad room is worth convening. A city adding centres this quickly has a specific set of problems: standing up a site while the talent market is being competed for, moving from support work to global ownership, and holding on to the people who did the first build. Those are the conversations on the agenda.
