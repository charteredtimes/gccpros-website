Community charter

A professional institution, not a commercial platform.

The GCC Industry Council is a peer community of professionals in full-time employment across the world’s Global Capability Centres. Membership carries no commercial obligation. The Council does not represent any employer and operates as an independent, non-commercial knowledge-sharing and advocacy body.

Peer-to-peer only

No consulting engagements, vendor relationships or commercial introductions.

Employer-neutral

Members take part as individuals, not as representatives of their organisations.

Members-only discretion

Discussions are shared among members in good faith and stay in the room.

Questions

Before you apply.

Who can join the GCC Industry Council? +

Serving leaders of Global Capability Centres who are in full-time employment at a GCC. Vendors, consultants and service providers are not eligible.

Is membership really free? +

Yes. The membership is valued at ₹5,00,000 a year and is complimentary for serving GCC leaders, for as long as you hold a GCC leadership role.

Will my identity or my centre’s data be published? +

No. Your identity stays masked. Only a Peer-Verified mark appears against your centre’s record, and sector benchmarks are research-only.

Do members represent their employers? +

No. Members take part as individual professionals. The Council does not represent any employer and carries no commercial obligation.

How is the Council different from GCC Insiders? +

The Council is for serving GCC leaders. GCC Insiders is the open community for GCC professionals at every level.
