Our data

How the numbers are built

Every centre is verified individually, with up to 46 fields per centre. A GCC with offices in more than one city is counted in each of those cities; every city, region and country total counts each GCC only once.

Every figure on this site comes from one source: the live database. A nightly job rebuilds the atlas, the city pages and every number quoted on every page from it, and will not publish if any page disagrees. When you read a GCC count here, it matches the database as of the date shown. How many GCCs are there in India?
