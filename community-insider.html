What the GCC Inner Circle is

Welcome to the GCC Inner Circle, the part of GCCPROs built for the practitioners rather than the platforms.

GCC Insiders is the open community that sits alongside the invite-only GCC Industry Council. Where the Council is limited to the leaders who run a centre, the Inner Circle is open to anyone who has actually worked inside a Global Capability Centre in India, from analyst to vice-president, across finance, technology, operations, human resources and sales.

Everyone is verified by hand before they are let in. That single rule is what keeps the room useful: no recruiters working a list, no vendors pitching a platform, no consultants prospecting. Just practitioners comparing notes on the problems GCCs actually have.

Who should join

Current GCC employees

Anyone on the payroll of a captive centre in India, at any level and in any function.

GCC alumni

People who have moved on but built their craft inside a capability centre and still think like an operator.

Functional specialists

Finance, technology, operations, HR and sales leads who own a process end to end for a global parent.

People weighing a move

Professionals deciding whether a GCC is the right next step, who want a straight answer from people already there.

What members get

  • Peer knowledge exchange: ask the questions you cannot ask a vendor, and get answers from people running the same playbook.
  • Industry updates: what is actually changing in India’s GCC landscape, drawn from the GCC database and our original research.
  • Early access to events: member invitations to the GCC Leadership Summits and closed-door roundtables.
  • A real network: practitioners across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, the NCR and beyond.

How verification works

Fill in the form above with your name, your current city and the GCC you work at or came from. Our team checks the details by hand against the centres in our database, which usually takes a working day or two. If anything is unclear we will write to you before deciding; we would rather ask than turn away a genuine practitioner.

Your details are never published and never sold. Read the privacy policy for exactly what we store and why. If you run a GCC rather than work in one, apply to the GCC Industry Council instead. It is a different room, with its own benchmark console.
